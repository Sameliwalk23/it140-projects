Haunted Mansion Escape

The player is trapped inside a haunted mansion and must collect six magical objects before facing the Ghost King. The mansion contains several rooms connected by hallways. The player moves from room to room collecting items that will help defeat the Ghost King. If the player encounters the Ghost King before collecting all six items, they lose the game. The player wins by collecting all items and then entering the Throne Room where the Ghost King is located.
